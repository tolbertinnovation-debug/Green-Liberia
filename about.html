Voices for a Green Liberia (VGL) Media Inc. is an environmental media and advocacy organization that uses journalism, broadcasting, digital communication, public education, and community engagement to promote environmental protection and sustainable development in Liberia.

What we believe

Protecting Liberia
is everyone’s work.

Six groups share the responsibility, and none of them can carry it alone.

VGL believes that environmental protection is not the responsibility of government alone. It requires informed citizens, responsible businesses, empowered communities, young people, civil society organizations, and strong institutional partnerships.

02Responsible businesses

Our vision

To become Liberia’s leading environmental media and public-engagement platform, inspiring citizens and institutions to protect the environment and build sustainable, healthy, and resilient communities.

Our mission

Educate. Engage. Empower.

Credible journalism and practical action, in the same organization.

To educate, engage, and empower Liberians through credible environmental journalism, public education, advocacy, community outreach, and practical environmental action.

Our environmental objectives

Ten objectives.
One environment.

The commitments VGL Media Inc. was founded to pursue.

01Increase public understanding of environmental issues affecting Liberia
02Promote responsible waste management and environmental sanitation
03Raise awareness about climate change and climate resilience
04Encourage protection of Liberia’s forests, wetlands, mangroves, rivers, beaches, and biodiversity
05Promote community participation in environmental protection
06Use media to amplify environmental policies, programs, and solutions
07Encourage young people to become environmental ambassadors
08Provide platforms for environmental experts, policymakers, communities, and citizens
09Promote partnerships between government, businesses, communities, and civil society
10Encourage practical, measurable environmental action
