What we do

Six areas,
one objective.

Each area feeds the others: what is reported gets discussed, what is discussed gets acted on, and what is acted on gets documented.

01

Environmental journalism

We report and produce stories that highlight environmental challenges, community concerns, government interventions, private-sector initiatives, and practical solutions.

02

VGL uses broadcast platforms to facilitate environmental discussions, interviews, awareness campaigns, expert conversations, and public education.

Two halves of the same work

Media reach and
physical action.

Awareness that never leaves the airwaves changes nothing on the ground; work on the ground that nobody hears about changes nothing in policy.

In the studio.

Broadcast is where an environmental issue becomes a public conversation — with experts, institutions, community leaders, and members of the public in the same discussion. The Environmental Hour is our dedicated programme for it.

The Environmental Hour ↗

On the street.

Community engagement is the other half: sanitation and clean-up activities, waste reduction, and citizen participation — with young people and residents doing the work alongside us.
